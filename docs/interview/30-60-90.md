# 30 / 60 / 90 day plan

## Days 1-30: learn, map, and earn trust
- Meet P&T: understand the landing zone, vending process, policies, Entra group model, hub networking.
- Inventory what AI Advisory runs today (client builds, where they live, how they deploy, secrets).
- Write the first ADRs (tenancy, network, identity) and review them with P&T *before* building.
- Quick win: stand up the internal ring-0 client (`chatham`) end to end with the stamp.

## Days 31-60: first hosted client on the platform
- Agree the vending template and SLA with P&T.
- Onboard the first real client (e.g., the insurance client) through the pipeline, in landing-zone mode.
- CI: PR checks, per-client plans, ring rollout, drift detection.
- Runbooks for onboarding, offboarding, and the top two incidents.

## Days 61-90: make it repeatable and hand-off-able
- Second client onboarded by someone else following the runbook (the real test of repeatability).
- Central ops workbook and alerts; cost per client reported monthly.
- Self-hosted runners in the platform VNet; close the state-account public endpoint gap.
- Write the platform "product" one-pager for the AI Advisory team: what you get, how to ask, SLAs.
- Plan the next hire: what they own, what they'll need on day one.
